THE STOMBFUN
MARKETPLACE.

A place to trade every token minted through StombFun — still non-custodial, still wallet-signed. No order gets filled without both sides signing their own half of the transaction; nothing ever sits in a StombFun-controlled wallet.

How trading will work

01

List or browse

Sellers set a price and sign a partial PSBT offering their runes. Buyers browse live listings, same as any order book — but the "book" is just PSBTs.

02

Complete the swap

A buyer's wallet fills in the other half — their BTC input and receiving output — completing one atomic transaction.

03

Sign, burn, settle

One signature broadcasts it. A slice of the trade is routed to buy back & burn STOMB — every trade quietly shrinks supply.
