Why hold STOMB

↻

Buyback & burn

A share of platform fees — mint fees today, marketplace fees later — buys back STOMB from the market and burns it. Supply only goes down.

☑

Curation & vote

Holders vote on which tokens launched through StombFun get featured on the homepage — the community picks what gets visibility.

⛓

Platform token

STOMB is StombFun's native token — every future token creation flows through it in one way or another, the way $LAUNCH anchors a pump.fun-style platform.

Roadmap — these mechanics activate as the create-a-token builder and marketplace ship, not live yet on the mint you see above.

How it works

01

Connect your wallet

StombFun talks directly to your UniSat wallet. Nothing is custodied — your keys never leave your device.

02

Build the transaction

The page builds a real Bitcoin transaction on the CRC-20 standard — a CRC-20 output, your postage, and the mint fee.

03

Sign & broadcast

Your wallet shows the exact amounts before you sign. One signature mints and sends — no third-party step.
