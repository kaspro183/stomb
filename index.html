How it works

01

You mint a crc-20 e

Every STOMB mint sends a Bitcoin transaction carrying on crc-20 (an OP_RETURN message following the crc-20 protocol) that references the STOMB etching and mints your allocation directly to your own wallet. That crc-20 is what public indexers read to recognize your balance (see the risk notice below for what this means for CRC-20 branding).

02

You pay the network

No contract collects funds. You only pay the Bitcoin transaction fee and, where applicable, a service fee from the launchpad.

03

Indexers validate

Your STOMB balance is recognized by public crc-20 indexers (unisat, ordinals.com) as soon as your mint transaction is confirmed on-chain.

Roadmap

Phase 2

Indexer & holders

Live holder table and supply tracking via a verified third-party crc-20 indexer.

List STOMB on existing Ordinals and crc-20 marketplaces so holders can trade once minted out.

Phase 4

Launchpad opens

Wire the "Create a Token" flow so other creators can deploy their own tokens through STOMB Launchpad.

Risk disclosure: STOMB is an experimental CRC-20 token (built on the crc-20 standard) with no protocol-level enforcement, no guarantee of value, liquidity, or exchange listing. The launch fee is a service fee, not an investment contract. Only mint with funds you can afford to lose. Do your own research.
