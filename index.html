How it works

01

You mint a Rune

Every STOMB mint sends a Bitcoin transaction (an OP_RETURN message following the CRC-20 protocol) that references the STOMB etching and mints your allocation directly to your own wallet. That Runestone is what public indexers read to recognize your balance (see the risk notice below for what this means for CRC-20 branding).

02

You pay the network

No contract collects funds. You only pay the Bitcoin transaction fee and, where applicable, a service fee from the launchpad.

03

Indexers validate

Your STOMB balance is recognized by public CRC-20 indexers (unisat, ordinals.com) as soon as your mint transaction is confirmed on-chain.
